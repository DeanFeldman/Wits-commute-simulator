# Level 1 Parking Layout Contract

Read this document before changing Level 1 geometry, parking rows, entrances, roads, cameras, or vehicle placement. It records the current, reference-approved layout and the assumptions that keep gameplay and scenery aligned.

The source of truth remains the code:

- `src/levels/ParkingLevel.js` owns playable parking dimensions, generated spaces, markings, parked cars, the target, spawn, cameras, and gameplay.
- `src/levels/parking/ParkingEnvironment.js` owns the lot footprint, surrounding roads/buildings, entrances, curbs, fences, boom gates, scenery, and their colliders.
- `test/parking-entrance-scenery.test.js` protects the layout's important dimensions and alignments.

## Coordinate and Scale Conventions

- Treat one Three.js world unit as approximately one metre.
- `Y` is up. The playable surface is near `Y = 0`.
- In the top-down sky view, north/the M1 is `-Z`, the lower campus street is `+Z`, west/the ARM building is `-X`, and Yale Road is `+X`.
- A vehicle rotation of `0` faces north (`-Z`). `Math.PI / 2` and `-Math.PI / 2` face across the lot.
- Do not rotate the whole level. The skew belongs to the lot boundary and selected rows only.

## Design Intent

Level 1 is based on the real Wits third-year parking area beside the ARM building and M1. Preserve these recognizable features:

- An irregular, tapered lot rather than a square rectangle.
- A north boundary that slopes south toward the east.
- A short stepped service edge on the west.
- Six long north-south double parking strips.
- Consistent drive aisles between the strips.
- A horizontal/parallel-parked row following the north curb.
- A gentler angled single row following the east curb.
- A lower street with a west player entrance and a separate central Entrance 9 checkpoint.
- Dense parking with only a small number of empty spaces.

Do not add directional arrows to the lot. Dashed centre markings are intentional.

## Standard Dimensions

These exported constants in `ParkingLevel.js` define the shared parking scale:

| Item | Value |
| --- | ---: |
| Bay width | 2.5 m |
| Bay length | 5.0 m |
| Drive aisle width | 6.0 m |
| Parking line width | 0.08 m |
| North-south slot pitch | 2.6 m |

The six double-row centres are 16 m apart. Each strip occupies 10 m across its two opposing 5 m bays, leaving a 6 m drive aisle. Preserve that relationship when moving rows.

## Lot Footprint

`PARKING_LAYOUT.mainLot` has a nominal centre of `(0, -8)`, width `122`, and depth `84`. Its authoritative top-down outline is:

```text
(-55, -50) -> (59, -38) -> (54, 34) -> (-61, 34)
-> (-61, -33) -> (-56, -33) -> (-56, -42)
-> (-61, -42) -> (-61, -50)
```

The asphalt is deliberately rendered as three convex `ShapeGeometry` pieces at `Y = 0.035`. This avoids the triangulation/clipping artefacts previously caused by one concave polygon. If the outline changes, keep the pieces convex and visually continuous.

## Parking Row Generation

All spaces are generated by `createParkingRow`, `createDoubleParkingRow`, and `getLevelOneParkingSpaces`. Do not hand-place hundreds of individual bays.

### Interior double strips

| Strip | Centre X | North start Z | South end Z |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| row-a | -42.5 | -38.9 | 32 |
| row-b | -26.5 | -37.2 | 32 |
| row-c | -10.5 | -35.5 | 25 |
| row-d | 5.5 | -33.8 | 25 |
| row-e | 21.5 | -32.1 | 28 |
| row-f | 37.5 | -30.5 | 26 |

The northern starts step south from west to east so the upper lane follows the sloped M1 boundary. The differing southern ends reflect the real lot and reserve turning/entrance space. Row A and B extend close to the west entrance curbs to provide the extra southern bays requested in the reference markup.

### North row

- Name: `top-row`
- Start: `(-50, -48.2)`
- Step: `(5.15, 0.542)`
- Count: `20`
- Rotation: `atan2(5.15, 0.542)`, approximately 84 degrees

This is parallel parking: each car's long axis runs horizontally along the north curb. Do not turn these into perpendicular bays. The slight row slope follows the actual boundary.

### West edge

- `west-upper`: `X = -58.2`, from `Z = -45.5` to `-37.7`
- `west-row`: `X = -58.25`, from `Z = -32.5` to `26`

These rows are intentionally close to the curb. Do not reintroduce a strip of unused asphalt between the parking and west boundary.

### East edge

- Start: `(56.6, -30)`
- Step: `(-0.19, 2.8)`
- Count: `21`
- Rotation: `-45 degrees`

This row follows the narrowing east boundary. Keep it close to the curb and avoid returning to the earlier, more aggressive `-60 degree` zigzag.

The current generator produces 363 total spaces before seeded vacancies are applied.

## Drive Aisles

The north-south aisle centre X positions are:

```text
-50.7, -34.5, -18.5, -2.5, 13.5, 29.5, 45.5
```

Each is 6 m wide. Their northern and southern extents are staggered to match the parking strips and skewed boundary. The upper cross-lane is 5.2 m deep, with its centre moving from `Z = -40.4` in the west to `Z = -30.2` in the east.

When changing a row, check both edges of every adjacent aisle. A row move should not make one lane noticeably wider or narrower than the others.

## Entrances, Spawn, and Boom Gates

### West player entrance

- Drive aisle centre: `X = -34.5`
- Asphalt throat: 7 m wide and continuous from the lower street into the lot
- Boundary opening: 17 m wide, including the two curb shoulders
- Player spawn: `(-34.5, 0, 41)`
- Player heading: `0`, facing north into the parking lot

The entrance is flanked by slightly elevated concrete curbs. They have `parking-entrance-curb` colliders; the normal Level 1 collision response stops the car and removes condition. The entrance boom is red/white, remains visible/down at spawn, and raises when the player rolls close to it. Keep the driving surface unobstructed between the street and aisle.

### Central entrance/checkpoint

- Main entrance centre: `X = -2.5`, width `9`
- Campus gate/checkpoint centre: `X = -2.5`

This is the Entrance 9-style canopy/checkpoint and is separate from the player's west spawn entrance. Do not merge the two without an explicit design request.

## Target and Parking Validation

The playable cyan target is `row-d-left`, index `17`, currently centred at approximately `(3, 10.4)` and rotated `-90 degrees`.

Success still requires all three checks:

1. sufficient vehicle containment in the target bay;
2. alignment within the configured tolerance;
3. near-zero speed for the confirmation period.

Moving the target or its row requires checking containment geometry, approach space, spawn route, and collision clearance—not only the visible cyan outline.

## Cars and Performance

- The player car uses `./assets/models/vehicles/car_scene.glb` through `attachPlayerCarModel`.
- Parked cars currently use seeded, low-poly instanced body/cabin meshes for browser performance.
- Approximately 8% of non-target bays are left empty by a deterministic seeded random sequence.
- Parked-car colliders are approximately `2.05 x 1.2 x 4.15`, safely inside a standard bay.
- Parking lines use two `InstancedMesh` batches. Preserve instancing or equivalent batching when adding spaces.
- Do not create repeated geometry or materials inside the update loop.

## Surface, Lighting, and Sky

- The lot uses the custom damaged/wet asphalt shader from `src/shaders/asphaltShader.js` and textures under `./assets/textures/road/`.
- Runtime asset paths must stay relative because the game is deployed from a subdirectory.
- Level 1 deliberately uses a light-blue sky (`0x8ec9ee`), not an HDRI skybox.
- Chase view uses matching fog. Sky view removes fog while active and restores it when returning to chase view.
- The sky camera is orthographic, centred over the lot at `Y = 90`, with `skyViewScale = 1.65`.
- The level must always start in chase view. Do not let the sky-view toggle alter the starting camera state.

## Collision and Boundary Rules

- Fences, curbs, buildings, parked cars, and a closed boom use `CollisionWorld`.
- A normal solid collision restores the previous car position, stops the vehicle, and removes 5 condition after cooldown.
- The car is allowed onto the lower street, but its broad movement clamp remains bounded to the Level 1 play area.
- Parking rows should reach their intended curbs, but neither vehicles nor bay markings should cross the physical boundary.

## Safe Editing Checklist

Before completing a Level 1 layout change:

1. Update layout constants rather than hand-placing equivalent objects.
2. Keep all drive aisles near the 6 m standard.
3. Preserve the parallel north row and gentler east row.
4. Verify the west spawn, asphalt throat, curb colliders, and boom still align at `X = -34.5`.
5. Check that the target remains reachable and valid.
6. Inspect both chase and sky views; confirm Level 1 starts in chase view.
7. Check for asphalt gaps, grass triangles, Z-fighting, curb gaps, or parking lines crossing walls.
8. Run `npm test` and `npm run build`.
9. Load all three levels and check the browser console.
